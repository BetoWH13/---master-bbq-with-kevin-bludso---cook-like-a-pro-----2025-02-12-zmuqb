About BBQ Masters

Founded in 2018 by renowned pitmaster Kevin Bludso, BBQ Masters emerged from a passion for authentic, mouth-watering barbecue and a desire to share decades of expertise with aspiring grill masters worldwide. What started as local workshops in Los Angeles has grown into a comprehensive online platform, helping thousands of students master the art of BBQ.

Our mission is to preserve and promote the authentic traditions of BBQ while making professional-level techniques accessible to everyone. We believe in the power of great food to bring people together and create lasting memories. Through our carefully crafted curriculum, hands-on approach, and commitment to excellence, we're building a global community of confident and skilled BBQ enthusiasts.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to complete the course?

The complete course can be finished in 6-8 weeks, but you'll have lifetime access to review and practice at your own pace. Each module is designed to build upon previous lessons while allowing flexibility for your schedule.

What equipment do I need to get started?

You'll need a basic grill or smoker, but Kevin provides recommendations for all budget levels. The course covers techniques for various equipment types, from entry-level to professional-grade smokers.

Is this course suitable for beginners?

Absolutely! The course starts with fundamentals and progressively moves to advanced techniques. Complete beginners will find clear, step-by-step instruction while experienced grillers will discover new professional insights.

Do I get personal feedback on my progress?

Yes! You'll have access to our community forum where Kevin and other BBQ experts provide feedback on your cooks. Monthly live Q&A sessions are also included for personal guidance.

What happens after I complete the course?

You'll maintain lifetime access to all course materials, updates, and our BBQ community. Many graduates go on to start catering businesses or compete in BBQ competitions with our ongoing support.
